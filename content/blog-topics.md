# Blog Post Topics for SEO

## Top-of-Funnel (Awareness / High Volume)

1. **Sleep Apnea Symptoms: 10 Warning Signs You Shouldn't Ignore** — Targets one of the highest-volume sleep queries. Natural CTA to find a clinic.

2. **Sleep Apnea in Women: Why It's Often Misdiagnosed** — Underserved topic with growing search interest; good featured-snippet potential.

3. **The Link Between Sleep Apnea and Heart Disease: What the Research Shows** — Builds medical authority, targets health-anxious searchers.

4. **Insomnia vs. Sleep Apnea: How to Tell the Difference** — Comparison query that captures people early in their diagnostic journey.

5. **Signs Your Child May Have a Sleep Disorder** — Pediatric angle parents actively search for; low competition.

6. **How Untreated Sleep Apnea Affects Your Brain, Heart, and Metabolism** — Consequence-driven content that motivates action (and clinic visits).

## Mid-Funnel (Consideration / Treatment Research)

7. **CPAP Alternatives: Every Modern Treatment Option for Sleep Apnea in 2026** — Extremely high search volume; people actively exploring solutions.

8. **Oral Appliance Therapy vs. CPAP: Pros, Cons, and Who Each Is Best For** — Comparison content that ranks well and has strong commercial intent.

9. **CBT-I for Insomnia: How It Works and How to Find a Provider** — Growing interest in non-medication approaches; ties to directory.

10. **Home Sleep Test vs. In-Lab Sleep Study: Which One Do You Need?** — Directly answers a common pre-appointment question.

11. **Narcolepsy: Symptoms, Diagnosis Path, and Finding the Right Specialist** — Disorder-specific long-tail with loyal search audience.

12. **Restless Legs Syndrome: When It's Time to See a Sleep Specialist** — Captures a specific disorder audience that needs clinic referrals.

## Bottom-of-Funnel (Decision / High Intent)

13. **How to Choose the Right Sleep Clinic: 7 Questions to Ask Before You Book** — Directly funnels to directory. Targets "how to choose a sleep clinic."

14. **What to Expect During a Sleep Study: A Step-by-Step Guide** — Targets anxious patients ready to book; high volume, evergreen.

15. **How Much Does a Sleep Study Cost? With and Without Insurance** — One of the highest commercial-intent queries in sleep medicine.

16. **Does Insurance Cover Sleep Studies? What You Need to Know by Plan Type** — Practical, high-intent query that pairs naturally with clinic search.

17. **Why AASM Accreditation Matters When Choosing a Sleep Center** — Ties directly to existing accreditation page; builds trust signals.

18. **What Is a Board-Certified Sleep Medicine Physician (and Why It Matters)?** — Establishes authority, differentiates quality clinics in directory.

## Trend / Local SEO Plays

19. **Telemedicine for Sleep Disorders: What Can Be Done Remotely and What Can't** — Trending topic; captures "online sleep doctor" queries.

20. **A State-by-State Look at Sleep Health in America: Where Are the Gaps?** — Linkable asset with data visualization potential. Each state section creates internal linking opportunities to directory filtered by state.

## Notes

- Posts 13–18 have the highest conversion potential (people already looking for a clinic).
- Posts 1–6 drive the most raw traffic and build domain authority.
- Post 20 is designed as a "linkable asset" for earning backlinks.
- Each post should end with a CTA like "Find an accredited sleep clinic near you" linking to the directory with relevant filters pre-applied.
